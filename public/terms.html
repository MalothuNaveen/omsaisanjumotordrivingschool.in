Terms & Conditions

Last updated 1 August 2026

1. Booking and confirmation

Submitting the booking form or messaging us on WhatsApp is an enquiry, not a confirmed booking. A coordinator will contact you within one working day to confirm your instructor, slot and pickup point. Your booking is confirmed only once we have said so in writing.

2. Payment

Course fees are payable after your slot is confirmed and before your first lesson. Prices shown on the website include the training vehicle, fuel and instructor time. Government fees for the learner’s licence (LLR), driving licence and RTO test are not included and are paid directly by you.

3. Lesson validity

The course must be completed within 45 days of the date you join. Access to remaining lessons ends once the 45-day period is over, unless an extension has been agreed with us in writing.

4. Rescheduling and cancellation

  • Reschedule free of charge with at least 12 hours’ notice.
  • Lessons cancelled with less than 12 hours’ notice, or missed without notice, are treated as used.
  • If we cancel a lesson for any reason — vehicle, instructor or weather — it is rescheduled at no cost to you.
  • Course fees are non-refundable once paid, including for lessons not yet taken.

5. Eligibility and documents

Learners must be of the minimum age required by law for the vehicle category being taught and must bring valid photo identification. To drive on public roads you must hold a valid learner’s licence (LLR). Practice on private grounds may begin before that.

6. Conduct and safety

Lessons are conducted in dual-control vehicles under instructor supervision at all times. You agree to follow your instructor’s directions, wear a seat belt, and not to drive under the influence of alcohol, drugs or medication that affects driving. We may end a lesson immediately, without refund, where safety is at risk.

7. Vehicles and insurance

Our training vehicles are insured for instructional use with a certified instructor present. Damage caused by wilful misuse or by driving without the instructor present is not covered and remains your responsibility.

8. Test results

We prepare you thoroughly for the RTO test, including mock tests, but we cannot guarantee a pass. Test outcomes are decided solely by the examining authority.

9. Liability

Nothing in these terms limits our liability where it cannot lawfully be limited. Otherwise our liability in connection with a course is limited to the amount you paid for it.

10. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to contact@omsaisanjumotordrivingschool.in or +91 90001 11516.
