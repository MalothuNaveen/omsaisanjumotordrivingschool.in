Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

1. Who we are

Om Sai Sanju Motor Driving School, Srinivasa Nagar Colony, Temple Alwal, Alwal, Secunderabad, Telangana 500010. You can reach us at contact@omsaisanjumotordrivingschool.in or on +91 90001 11516.

2. What we collect

When you submit a booking enquiry we collect only what we need to arrange lessons:

  • Your name, phone number and (optionally) email address
  • The course, transmission, area, date and time you prefer
  • Your current driving experience and any notes you choose to add
  • Your pickup address, if you request pickup

3. Why we use it

To contact you about your enquiry, confirm your instructor and slot, issue receipts, and keep a record of your progress across lessons. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

Only the instructor assigned to you and the coordinator managing your booking. We share information with government authorities such as the RTO only where you have asked us to assist with a licence application, or where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for 12 months. Records of completed courses are kept for 3 years so that we can reissue certificates and confirm training history on request.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by emailing contact@omsaisanjumotordrivingschool.in. You can also ask us to stop contacting you, and we will close the enquiry.

7. Cookies

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Any cookies used are strictly necessary for the site to function.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated to learners with an active booking.
